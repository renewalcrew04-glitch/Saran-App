SARAN – Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Renewalcrew Technologies Private Limited (“SARAN”, “we”, “us”, “our”) operates the SARAN mobile applications and related services (the “Platform”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use SARAN.

We process personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), and applicable Indian regulations. By using SARAN, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data controller

Renewalcrew Technologies Private Limited is the data controller for personal data processed through the SARAN Platform. Contact: hello@saranapp.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account and identity

  • Email address
  • Name and username
  • Password (stored in encrypted form)
  • Verification-related data (e.g. for eligibility and safety), processed only for that purpose

2.2 Profile and content

  • Profile photo, cover image, bio, and location (if you choose to add them)
  • Posts, S-Frames (stories), comments, likes, reposts
  • Direct messages and shared content
  • Journal entries, wellness trackers (e.g. period, hydration), and in-app game usage, where you use these features

2.3 Safety and SOS

  • Precise location when you use the SOS feature, to share with trusted contacts or nearby SARAN users within the chosen radius
  • Trusted contact details you add for SOS alerts
  • Reports and blocks (e.g. reported content, blocked accounts) for moderation and safety

2.4 Device and usage

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Log data (e.g. access times, features used) for security and improving the service

3. How we use your data

  • Provide the service: To create and manage your account, show your profile and content, enable posts, S-Frames, events, DMs, and wellness features.
  • Safety: To operate the SOS feature, share location with your chosen contacts when you trigger SOS, and to moderate content (review reports, enforce Community Guidelines).
  • Verification and eligibility: To maintain a women-only, adult (18+) environment in line with our Terms.
  • Support and compliance: To respond to you, handle grievances, and comply with applicable law.
  • Improve the Platform: To fix issues, analyse usage in aggregated form, and improve features. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or tracking.

4. Legal basis (where applicable)

We process your data where necessary to perform our contract with you (Terms of Service), to comply with legal obligations, for our legitimate interests (safety, security, moderation, improving the service), and, where required, with your consent (e.g. for optional features or sensitive data). You may withdraw consent where it is the basis, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only:

  • With other users as you choose (e.g. profile, posts, DMs, SOS alerts to trusted contacts or nearby users when you use SOS)
  • With service providers who assist us (e.g. hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality and data protection obligations
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property (e.g. lawful requests from authorities, enforcement of our Terms)

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, enforce our Terms, and comply with law. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data in line with our retention policy, except where we must retain it for legal or safety reasons.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data (e.g. encryption, access controls, secure infrastructure). No system is completely secure; we ask you to keep your password and account details safe.

8. Your rights

Under applicable law (including the DPDP Act), you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (e.g. via Edit Profile in the app)
  • Request deletion of your data (e.g. via Delete Account in Settings)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Grievance redressal (see below)

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@saranapp.com or use in-app options (e.g. Settings → Delete Account for full account deletion).

9. Children

SARAN is for adults (18+) only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the Platform, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

10. Grievance officer

In accordance with Indian law, we have appointed a Grievance Officer:

Email: support@saranapp.com
Response time: Within seven (7) working days.

You may raise complaints or queries about this Privacy Policy or our data practices to the Grievance Officer.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of SARAN after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we may notify you in the app or by email where appropriate.
